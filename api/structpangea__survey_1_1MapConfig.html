    Detailed Description

    Fully parsed settings shared by orthomap and XYZ export.

    Distances are metres, coordinates are WGS-84 degrees, and image dimensions are pixels. lowMemory describes a process policy, not measured whole-board headroom or a hard resident-memory limit.
