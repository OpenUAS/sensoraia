    Detailed Description

    Convert validated USHER AI Camera evidence into GREAT source records.

    Inputs must be real bounded JPEGs with complete EXIF, profile-matched dimensions, and qualified pose evidence. Synthetic map imagery and nominal lens-only geometry are rejected rather than promoted into survey sources.
