    Detailed Description

    USHER-side audio supervision, pose association and immutable EAR snapshots.

    A persistent EARcam child produces newline-delimited acoustic measurements. A reader thread retains only the latest measurement while USHER geotags it at shots; the bounded session can be detached without copying for the backend's finalizer. Numerical fusion and JPEG rendering live in the standalone EAR core, not USHER's serial loop. Missing microphones remain nonfatal and are retried. Thread ownership: the supervisor owns child lifecycle, the reader publishes latest under ear_mutex, the serial owner handles sessions/pending poses/log enqueue, and the log worker alone accesses its FILE. The finalizer borrows detached snapshots until release; it must not touch active-session APIs.
