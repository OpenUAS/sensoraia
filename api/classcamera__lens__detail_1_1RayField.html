    Detailed Description

    Full-frame ray field: exact inverse distortion on a coarse node grid, bilinear interpolation between nodes.

    Brown and fisheye inversion is iterative and dominated rendering time when evaluated per pixel and per colour. The distortion field is smooth, so the node step is halved until every interpolated cell-centre ray forward-projects within ray_interpolation_tolerance_px of its pixel. Step 1 is the exact per-pixel inverse.
