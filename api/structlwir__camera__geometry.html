    Detailed Description

    Projection calibration in native coordinates, never display coordinates.

    Lens coefficients follow OpenCV's pinhole/Brown-Conrady convention. Keeping them in the stored native pixel frame prevents display rotation or preview enlargement from silently changing the ray being projected.

    ◆ camera_to_body

    double lwir_camera_geometry::camera_to_body[9]

    Row-major rotation: camera right/down/forward to body forward/right/down.

    Must be a proper rotation (orthonormal, determinant +1); projection rejects anything else. The loader default {0,-1,0, 1,0,0, 0,0,1} maps camera forward to body down and camera up (-y) to body forward: a nadir camera with the image top toward the nose.
