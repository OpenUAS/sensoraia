    ◆ temporal_sampling()

    TemporalSampling camera_lens_detail::temporal_sampling ( const RayField & field,
    const Pose & pose,
    int width,
    int height,
    int maximum )

    Choose the fewest odd exposure samples that resolve motion blur.

    Parameters
    fieldReference-channel ray field.
    poseShot motion, vibration and shutter model.
    widthOutput width.
    heightOutput height.
    maximumProfile sample ceiling (odd).
    Returns
    Sample count whose neighbouring-sample ground spacing is at most temporal_sample_spacing_px at every probe, plus the largest blur path.

    Probe the corners, edge centres and centre of the frame, follow each ray's ground intersection across the exposure (including vibration reversals) and convert path length to pixels with the local ground sampling distance. Sub-pixel blur therefore costs few samples, while long exposures keep every sample the profile allows.
