    Detailed Description

    Apply and periodically check the survey process's low-memory policy.

    The address-space ceiling, current RSS, and system-available reserve are different measurements. Checking all three helps a job stop with a resumable checkpoint before it crowds the camera board, but does not prove whole-board safety or replace physical RSS/PSS/CMA/concurrency measurements.
