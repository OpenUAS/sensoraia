    Detailed Description

    Private storage for the synthetic field and its GP posterior.

    Keep truth beside the synthetic observation source, not in the network's inputs: the planner should learn through measurements, not peek at the answer sheet. The mean and full covariance evolve together in place.

    Invariant
    Only accepted increasing observation numbers advance replay state.
    Warning
    The covariance is dense. Additional copies are substantial allocations on MORA's shared 512 MB board, even when the public Belief stays small.

    Load the complete synthetic lattice without inventing missing truth.

    Numeric rows outside the deployed even-coordinate grid are ignored after parsing. Selected cells must appear exactly once; silently averaging duplicates or filling holes would change the experiment.

    Parameters

    [in]experimentImmutable GP and synthetic sensor policy.

    std::exceptionFor file-system, allocation, reading, or data-validation failures.
    Note
    The 2 MiB file and 256-character row limits bound accepted input, not the dense covariance or every transient allocation made by streams.
