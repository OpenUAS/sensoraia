    Detailed Description

    Encode simulated-camera pose, optics, and uncertainty into EXIF.

    Metadata distinguishes commanded pose from observed simulator pose, and labels nominal/emulated camera settings rather than presenting them as physical measurements. This is important because a complete EXIF record is provenance, not proof of exposure synchronization or surveyed accuracy.
